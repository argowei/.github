# Getting help

Start with the [project README](https://github.com/argowei/argowei) and [deployment guide](https://github.com/argowei/argowei/blob/main/docs/DEPLOYMENT.md). If they do not answer your question, open an issue in [argowei/argowei](https://github.com/argowei/argowei/issues/new/choose) with your setup and what you tried. Remove credentials and private network details from logs and screenshots.

For a suspected vulnerability, use the [security policy](SECURITY.md) instead of a public issue with technical details.
