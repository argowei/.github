### What Argowei does today

| Discover | Understand | Respond |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find devices on a configured local network and keep a persistent inventory. | Review names, presence history, device types, and changes over time. | Classify devices with groups and trust levels; acknowledge new-device alerts. |

Argowei is at an early stage. Service analysis, richer network signals, and infrastructure-based policy enforcement are directions for future work, not current capabilities. The [repository README](https://github.com/argowei/argowei) explains the current behavior and limitations.
